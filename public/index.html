02 — Studio

The studio,

Lumiere is a private, by-appointment studio built around a single idea — your time should feel like an exhale. We work in quiet rooms with linen, natural light, and the products we trust.

Our team of senior stylists has trained across Tokyo, Paris, and New York. The result is a calm, exact approach to hair that you carry with you for weeks.
